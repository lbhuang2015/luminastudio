Privacy Policy

Lumina 2048: Glass Prism

How the App collects, uses, and shares information — and why, for a small free puzzle game, the answer stays short.

LINBO HUANG (“we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information Lumina 2048: Glass Prism (the “App”) collects, uses, and shares when you use it.

The App is free to play. It contains no in-app purchases and no subscriptions, and it is supported entirely by advertising.

Information We Collect

The App itself does not require account registration and does not directly collect your name, email, or other personally identifying information. The following information is collected by third-party services integrated into the App or by Apple’s own system services:

Advertising Data (Google AdMob)

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display a bottom banner ad, and to show an optional rewarded video ad (see below). To serve ads, Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier, IDFA, only with your consent), approximate location, device model and OS information, and IP address, used for ad delivery, frequency capping, and performance analytics. This data is handled by Google under its own privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy. For details specifically on how Google uses data when you use apps that partner with Google, see How Google Uses Information from Sites or Apps that Use Our Services.

Rewarded Video Ads (Save / Load)

Saving and loading your game progress are optional convenience features unlocked by watching a short rewarded video ad. When you tap Save or Load, the App asks you to confirm first; only if you confirm and watch the video to completion is the save or load performed. You are never required to watch an ad to play the game itself, and you may cancel at any time. If your device is offline, the App skips the ad entirely and performs the save or load locally at no cost. Rewarded video ads are served by Google AdMob and involve the same categories of data described above.

Advertising Consent (Google UMP)

In regions where it is required (such as the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland), the App uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to present a consent form before any ad is requested, so you can choose how your data is used for advertising purposes. Your choice is stored on your device by the Google UMP SDK. You can reopen the form and change your choice at any time using the “Privacy Options” button at the bottom of the main screen, where that option is applicable.

App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On first launch, the App requests your permission via Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework to show more relevant ads. You may decline; the App remains fully usable and will still show ads (non-personalized, not based on cross-app tracking) if you decline. You can change this permission anytime under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See Apple’s About Tracking Transparency for more information on how this works.

Game Center Leaderboard

If you choose to sign in and use the leaderboard feature, the App submits your score to Apple’s Game Center service and displays the top 10 scores along with your rank. The App maintains a separate leaderboard for each board size (3×3, 4×4, 5×5). Your Game Center nickname/account information is managed by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy; the App does not separately store your Game Center account data.

Local Save Data and Settings

Your game progress (board state, score), your best scores for each board size, and your preferences (selected board size, sound on/off) are saved locally on your device (UserDefaults) and are never uploaded to any server — we do not operate a backend, and the App does not sync game data with any server. Uninstalling the App clears this local data.

What We Don’t Do

  • We do not collect your name, phone number, email, or other direct identifiers
  • We do not perform cross-app/cross-site tracking without ATT authorization
  • We do not sell your data to third parties
  • We do not offer in-app purchases and never handle payment information of any kind
03

Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us at the email below so we can remove it.

04

Your Choices and Rights

  • You can disable tracking permission or limit ad tracking anytime in iOS Settings
  • Where applicable, you can review and change your advertising consent choices at any time via the “Privacy Options” button in the App
  • You can decline to watch a rewarded video ad; doing so simply means the optional Save or Load action is not performed
  • You can clear local save data by uninstalling the App
  • To view or delete Game Center–related data, please use Apple’s official channels (Settings → Game Center), as that data is held by Apple
05

US State Privacy Rights (e.g., California CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a resident of California or another U.S. state with a comprehensive privacy law, you may have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request deletion of that information, to correct inaccurate information, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

We do not sell your personal information for money. However, Google AdMob’s collection of device identifiers for personalized advertising may be considered a “share” of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising under some state laws. You can opt out of this in either of the following ways:

  • Decline the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt on first launch, or turn tracking off later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking — this stops the App from sharing your advertising identifier for cross-app/cross-site tracking.
  • Where a “Privacy Options” control appears at the bottom of the main screen (shown when your region’s privacy regulations require it), use it to review and change your advertising consent choices, including limiting the use of your data for personalized advertising.

To exercise a right to know, delete, or correct your personal information, contact us at the email below. Because the App itself does not hold any directly identifiable personal information about you, requests concerning advertising data or Game Center data may need to be directed to Google or Apple respectively, and we will assist in pointing you to the right channel.
