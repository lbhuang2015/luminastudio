Gameplay Basics

How do I play?

Swipe up, down, left, or right to slide every tile on the board at once. Tiles with the same number merge into one when they collide, and your score goes up with every merge.

How do I change the board size?

Tap the gear icon in the bottom control bar to open Settings, then choose 3×3, 4×4, or 5×5. Changing the board size starts a new game — each size keeps its own save file and its own best score.

How do I restart or mute the game?

The bottom control bar has a Reset icon (starts a new game on your current board size) and a Mute icon (toggles sound effects on/off).

02

Save & Load

Save and Load are optional convenience features, unlocked by watching a short rewarded video ad.

Why does it ask me to watch a video? Tapping Save or Load shows a confirmation card first. If you confirm and watch the video to completion, the save or load goes through. You can cancel any time, and you’re never required to watch an ad just to keep playing.

What if I’m offline? If your device has no internet connection, the App skips the ad entirely and performs the save or load locally, for free.

Game Center & Leaderboards

The leaderboard says “Sign in to Game Center”

Open the iOS Settings app → Game Center, and sign in with your Apple ID. Once signed in, reopen the leaderboard in Lumina 2048 (tap the trophy icon in the score panel).

Why are there three separate leaderboards?

Each board size (3×3, 4×4, 5×5) has its own leaderboard, so scores are only ever compared against other players using the same board size.

The leaderboard won’t load / shows an error

This is usually a temporary network or Game Center service issue — check your connection and try again in a moment. If it keeps happening, email us with your device model and iOS version.

Ads & Privacy Choices

Lumina 2048 is completely free with no in-app purchases, supported by a bottom banner ad and the optional rewarded video described above.

  • To limit ad tracking, go to iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and turn off tracking for Lumina 2048
  • Where required (EEA, UK, Switzerland), you can revisit your advertising consent choice anytime via the “Privacy Options” button at the bottom of the main screen

Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

Troubleshooting

The game feels stuck or a swipe didn’t register

Make sure your swipe is a clear, single-direction motion across the board. If the app becomes unresponsive, close it from the App Switcher and relaunch — your last save is unaffected.

I lost my progress

Game state is saved locally on your device only; it is not backed up to any server. Uninstalling the App, or using Load without ever having used Save on that board size, will not restore progress from another device.
